A-BASE/, A-HEUR-01/ and A-R1/ contain evidence records. Missing IDs, absolute timestamps and uncertainty remain blank. Local hashes identify tested archives; screenshots cannot verify uploaded bytes. Generated archives and official decks remain local. Original Colab not_submitted flags were saved before manual uploads and are not rewritten.

Track repair attempts separately, preserve failed evidence and coordinate submission slots. A rating snapshot is not a final rank or a controlled paired evaluation result.
